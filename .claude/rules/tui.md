# Drawing the screens

## Conventions

- Every SGR comes from `src/tui/style.ts` (`ROLES` through `open`/`close` or `painter`, `RESET`/`FG_RESET`/`BG_RESET`/`INK_RESET`, `slotFg`/`slotBg`, `indexed`, and the content helpers `ansiFg`/`ansiBg`/`ansiBar`/`ansiSquares`) and, in the shell layer, from `TTHEME_SGR` in `shell/ttheme.zsh`; `style.test.ts` fails on any other — a template like `\x1b[${30 + n}m` included — holds the zsh table key for key to `ROLES` plus `reset`, `/fg`, `/bg`, `/ink` and `fg0`–`fg7`, checks that each role closes exactly what it opens (`closing(open(role)) === close(role)`, so a closer lists its codes in `closing`'s order), that slot 6 reads at 3:1 on every official background and `SURFACES` step away from it in order, and that `MARKS` holds no glyph twice. The shell layer's marks come from `TTHEME_MARKS` beside `TTHEME_SGR`, which the same test holds to `MARKS` key for key, failing on any mark the shell writes literally but ↑ ↓ × ■ · … (key names, counts and prose there) and on `✕`; a new mark in `MARKS` is banned from the shell by itself. CONTRIBUTING.md's "Drawing the screens" is the reference.
- Chrome against content decides the color: chrome is a role (the accent is slot 6, never a palette's cursor), content is the palette's hex. Content: swatches, a lit row's ▌ and selection bar (`PaletteList.rowText`, browse's `lit`, the editor's sidebar rows in the terminal's own colors), browse's detail title in the palette's cursor, preview's `gc` (the gutter, the cursor row's ■, the sample header), the scenes, the mock terminal and the mark `>ω` in the hub's tab bar and the editor's About box (`markOf`, `markLines`), which wear the signature of the palette the tab shows and fall back to the foreground and the accent where the screen does not know one. Chrome: tabs and badges (`tabOf`, `pillOf`), the current ◆ (preview's `cdot`, wrapped in `ac` where it is drawn), an active catalog, the ▶ and heavy frame of the part in focus, the editor's title ◆ and its choice pills.
- Preview builds its accent once a frame in `__tt_pv_draw`: `ac` (accent), `pl` (pill) and `hm` (match) are `TTHEME_SGR`'s, and while `applied` ≠ `painted` they carry the applied palette's ANSI 6 in truecolor, as `__tt_pv_foot`'s `y` carries its ANSI 3 — the terminal's slots still hold the palette painted before. `gc` and `sb` are the applied palette's cursor and selection, the lit row's content colors.
- `fit` and `clip` (`src/tui/ansi.ts`) end a cut with `closing(head)` — only what the kept text opened — never `RESET`, so a cut inside a painted row keeps its background; the runtime's own resets (`Screen`, `cover`) stay full ones.
- Browse and the palette editor draw their key bar with `barLine` (`src/tui/bar.ts`) and their notices through `toned` (ok, warn, error, plain for information) as that bar's lead with the keys kept after it; find keeps its own `foot`, led by its one `view.notice` in the same tones, and init's picker its hint line. Browse, the editor and find open `?` as one `Help` (`src/tui/help.ts`: topics with the key each runs, a filter, the screen's Marks legend, laid with `overlay`, which blanks the column beside the box where it holds another box's edge and takes the click zones off the screen under it but its last row), and every list a window cuts says so with `moreOf` — a screen grows none of these of its own. `mise run look kit:<part>` draws each of these parts in every state it takes (`tests/kit.ts`).
- Preview's `?` (`__tt_pv_help`) and its settings note (`__tt_pv_conf_panel`) wrap through `__tt_pv_wrap`, the zsh copy of `help.ts`'s `wrap` (at the `·` parts, a part too long for a line at its words); `help.test.ts` holds the two together — the lines each draws, cut to their width, over the help's own topics and kana and Han text at nine widths, the zsh function run as it stands in the file —, so a change to one fails until the other follows. A pane too short for every topic draws those that fit whole, then `$TTHEME_MARKS[below] n more`, and a note with no rows left ends in `$TTHEME_MARKS[cut]`. `__tt_pv_foot` writes from the second column and ends a column short of the window's edge, as the rest of preview's chrome and Browse's bar do — the sample, its heading and the edit panel end there too (`se`, `pw - 1`), with or without the sample beside the list, so the hub's two tabs share one right edge —, and drops the release line, then the note, then the apply and pin bars' `<palette> →` (`pre`), then keys from the end — never the last, and the first only after the key on the right —, its `pick` zones following the choices.
- Text someone else wrote (a marketplace's files, the index, a booru's answer, the names release, an error built from them) reaches a screen or a message with every control and direction character dropped or spelled out: `inert`, `inertLines`, `quoted` or `escaped` (`src/core/text.ts`) in Browse, the editor's notices, the readers and the CLI, `Line.put` and `linked` in find, `aliasesZsh` and the conf writers for the shell.
- `surfaceOf(ground, ink, name)` is the only mix a screen's chrome may draw, and only from colors the screen was told: the palette it painted, or `Look.chrome` from the OSC query, where `runEditor` fills a slot the terminal left unanswered from the palette it opened with (`editor.start`) and the selection from `surfaceOf(…, 'selection')`. Every supported terminal answers OSC 10 and 11; drawing the builder from roles alone when they do not would take a second rendering path, so it is not done.

## Gotchas

- In zsh, a `TTHEME_SGR` key that starts with `/` must not sit inside the pattern of `${var/pattern/repl}` — zsh takes the `/` in `[/dim]` for the separator even inside quotes ("bad pattern"); copy it to a local first (`dx=$TTHEME_SGR[/dim]`, then `${row//$dx/…}`, which zsh matches literally without `GLOB_SUBST`).
- `__tt_pv_draw` declares `local -i … mt=${#TTHEME_ORDER}`: a string local of the same name turns into a math error ("bad math expression: illegal character: ^[") that only the color path reaches, which the `NO_COLOR` screens never run. Name a new local in preview after grepping the function.
- `tests/screens/` are captured under `NO_COLOR` with `capture-pane -p`, so they hold the plain tier only. To check a change to color, run `mise run look <scenario> <size> --emit txt,styles --rows a-b` before and after and compare the runs' styles: every difference should be one the change meant.
